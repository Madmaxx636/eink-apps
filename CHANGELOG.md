# Changelog

## 1.0

First public release of all six apps.

- **Ink Pad**: word processor with headings, bold, italic, underline, strike, five sizes, three fonts, bullet and
  numbered lists, alignment, undo and redo, find and replace, and word count. Opens .docx, .odt, .html and text files
  and saves as .docx, .odt, PDF, HTML or text. Lock with a PIN or password, with fingerprint or face as a shortcut.
- **Weather**: current conditions, US warnings, next-24-hours table, daily rows that open a full day screen (every
  hour, plus humidity, dew point, pressure, cloud cover, visibility, sunrise and sunset, daylight and sunshine), air
  quality and pollen, and a still satellite picture from NOAA GOES (Americas and the Pacific) in natural colour,
  infrared or water vapour.
- **Clock**: live clock with seconds, alarms with repeat days, sound, vibration and snooze that ring over the lock
  screen, a timer, a stopwatch with tenths or hundredths, and a world clock.
- **Calculator**: basic and scientific keypad with memory, history, sign change, squares and reciprocals; a tip
  calculator; a currency converter on European Central Bank rates that are saved for offline use; and a unit
  converter for length, mass, volume, area, temperature, speed, time, data and energy.
- **Calendar**: month grid and agenda over the phone's calendars, read only.
- **Files**: Files, Recents and Storage tabs, a tappable path, favorites, sorting per folder, compact or detailed
  lists, copy, move, rename, delete, zip and unzip, search, SD cards and USB drives, home-screen shortcuts, and an
  optional PIN with fingerprint or face unlock.
- **All apps**: e-ink or normal mode chosen on first launch; pages with swipe, buttons or volume keys; button styles
  and positions; compact or comfortable sizing; and an About screen in Settings.
